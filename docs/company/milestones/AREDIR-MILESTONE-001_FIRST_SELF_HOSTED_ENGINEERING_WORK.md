# AREDIR-MILESTONE-001: First Self-Hosted Engineering Work

## Milestone

Aredir established and validated the Engineering Work domain in Neon Dev, including migration application, seed behavior, and project isolation. An authenticated user then created the first real AredirLabs.com Engineering Work record through the product. The record persisted, opened in the established project-scoped detail experience, did not replay or duplicate on refresh, and remained isolated to AredirLabs.com.

Before this point, Engineering Work was represented through code, migrations, and seed infrastructure. After it, an authenticated user could create it inside Aredir. This is the transition from representing engineering activity to beginning to operate Aredir development through that representation.

## Meaning and boundary

This milestone begins self-hosted Engineering Work intake; it does not claim that Aredir fully self-hosts all engineering activity or its full lifecycle.

Immediate dogfooding exposed real follow-on needs: updates, lifecycle or retirement behavior, richer intake context, priority and rationale, workflow-vocabulary gaps, and duplicate prevention or resolution. Those needs are evidence for future work, not capabilities claimed by this milestone.

## Follow-on validation

Aredir has now validated both authenticated Engineering Work creation and authenticated, project-scoped Engineering Work update through the application.
